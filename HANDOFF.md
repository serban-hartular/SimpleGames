## Game map and intended behavior

Each scene is in `src/game/scenes/`, with defaults in the corresponding
`src/game/config/*.ts` file.

- **ArtilleryGame**: angle controls repeat while held, fire a ballistic shot at a
  random target. Separate `objects/Terrain.ts` and `objects/Projectile.ts`.
  Dotted trajectory shows time-spaced samples; explosions and synthesized firing,
  flight, miss and hit audio.
- **DivisibilityGame**: numbered balloons, configurable sequence of divisors.
  No duplicate numbers on the board; range through 999, half below 100 and half
  at least 100. Wrong clicks pop with an X and add one divisible and one
  nondivisible balloon. Timed rounds, generated pop/buzz/victory sounds.
- **SnakeGame**: grid snake, wrap on both axes, apples never spawn on edges or
  snake; configurable simultaneous apples and speed. Click steering and arrow
  steering; `arrowKeysOnly` can disable click/touch. Crunch and turn sounds.
  Model: `logic/SnakeBoard.ts`. Current source defaults: 3 apples, speed 2,
  arrowKeysOnly false.
- **NavigationGame**: caravel on a coarse wind grid over finer generated land;
  `logic/NavigationMap.ts` and `logic/Navigation.ts`. Two configurable gyres,
  upper clockwise and lower anticlockwise. Land fineness defaults to 10; smaller,
  separated islands. Wind exists even in land-containing cells; arrows hidden
  only for wholly land cells. Continental coastal correction reduces lee shores.
  Ship bumps against land. Three coastal ports (upper/lower continent and island),
  all must be visited in any order; generous arrival areas and gyre-facing
  continental placement. Golden ring and sound on arrival. Ten seconds stuck
  triggers sinking message and restart. Map spans available width; HUD above/below.
- **FoxGame**: random connected maze, fox steals all chickens and pies, dogs
  patrol straight segments and chase on clear sightlines only within their
  patrol territory. Chickens wander/flee at separate speeds, with alert visuals.
  Dogs catching the fox ends the game. Counters below maze. Model:
  `logic/FoxBoard.ts`, visuals: `visuals/FoxFigures.ts`, sound: `audio/FoxSounds.ts`.

## Latest fox control behavior (important)

Arrows or click steer. Space or clicking the fox stops it immediately; other
actors keep moving. After a stop, steering in a different direction aligns the
fox to the nearest square and turns immediately, discarding the old segment.
If that turn is blocked by a wall, the fox stays stopped. Continuing in the same
direction resumes the existing segment. This deliberately fixes the reported
bug where it first moved a little in the previous direction before turning.

Current config includes `arrowKeysOnly: false`; when enabled, arrows and Space
still work, click/touch controls are disabled. Keyboard listeners/captures are
removed on scene shutdown. See `tests/fox-controls.cjs` and `tests/fox.cjs`.

Fox defaults: zoom 3, speeds fox 3 / chickenWander 1.4 / chickenFlee 2.3 /
dogPatrol 1.8 / dogChase 3.5; all counts auto; seed null.
Zoom 1–10 sets height to `7 + 2 * zoom`, width proportional and odd. Counts accept
`'auto'` or literal integers; auto counts scale to maze size, literals validated.

## Settings and localization

All five games have a shared HTML settings dialog: `settings/dialog.ts`, with
validation/persistence in `settings/store.ts`. Opening pauses gameplay/audio;
Cancel resumes; Apply & Restart validates, saves and restarts. Restore Defaults
fills the form, requiring Apply. JSON import/export and advanced asset fields
are supported. Persisted settings override source defaults; a source edit may
appear ineffective until Restore Defaults is applied. Existing saved settings
also need compatibility when adding new fields (see current store/tests).

Global language source default: `config/global.ts`, Romanian (`ro`). English
also available. Language can be selected on menu or in a game's settings and is
remembered globally. `i18n/ro.ts` and `en.ts` cover game/UI/settings strings;
`i18n/index.ts` registers languages, provides `t`, interpolation and fallback.
Add new languages by copying a catalog and registering its native display name.
Preserve keys and numbered placeholders; translate values. New user-facing
strings should be added to both catalogs. Internal scene keys/config values
must remain stable. Read README's Language section.
